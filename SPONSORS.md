# Sponsor BifrostNMS

BifrostNMS is being developed as an open-source Network Monitoring System with a commercial hosted **BifrostNMS Cloud** service planned alongside it.

Sponsorship helps fund development time, infrastructure, test environments, domains, signing/release infrastructure and the less glamorous maintenance work required to keep an open-source monitoring platform healthy.

## How to support the project

GitHub Sponsors is the intended primary sponsorship channel. Sponsorship tiers and links will be added once the project is ready to accept funding.

Until then, the most useful support is to star the repository, test early releases, report reproducible bugs, contribute code/documentation, and tell other network and infrastructure engineers about BifrostNMS.

## Organisations

If your organisation depends on BifrostNMS and would like to fund a feature, ongoing maintenance, or development of a probe/integration, please open a GitHub Discussion or contact the maintainer through the contact details on the GitHub profile.

## Principles

Sponsorship does not buy control over the open-source project. Security, maintainability, interoperability and the interests of the wider community remain the priorities. Sponsored work will be identified transparently where appropriate.

Thank you for helping build BifrostNMS.
